Overview
What Is FuelEU Pooling & Why It Matters

FuelEU Maritime sets a maximum annual GHG intensity for each ship's energy use. Ships that use cleaner fuels generate a compliance surplus (positive CB); those that don't generate a compliance deficit (negative CB). Pooling allows surplus from greener ships to offset deficits on legacy tonnage — either within the same company (internal pool) or across companies (external pool).

Done well, pooling is a cash and compliance optimisation tool. Done poorly — wrong timing, unverified data, or mis-matched commercial agreements — it results in penalties, rejected pools, and Document of Compliance failures for individual ships.

Core Formula
CB = (Itarget − Iactual) × Etotal / 106
CB = Compliance Balance (positive = surplus, negative = deficit)  ·  Itarget = GHG intensity target for the year (gCO₂e/MJ)  ·  Iactual = Ship's actual annual average WtW GHG intensity  ·  Etotal = Total energy used on board (MJ)

Commercial Strategy
Surplus vs Deficit — What to Do with Each
+CB
If you have SURPLUS
Use to offset own fleet's deficit ships first
Bank surplus for future years (requires separate verification)
Sell into external pools via a pool organiser / marketplace
Bundle with biofuel supply as a value-added compliance package
Price against penalty rate to find external sale threshold
−CB
If you have DEFICIT
First: pool with own surplus ships internally
Second: buy external surplus from pool marketplace
Third: switch to lower-intensity fuel to reduce deficit
Last resort: accept penalty — compare penalty vs purchase cost
Always: ensure deficit does not worsen post-pool
External Pool Decision Logic
Is internal surplus sufficient to offset all own deficits?
Yes No
YES → No external purchase needed. Evaluate whether to sell residual surplus or bank it.
NO → Calculate shortfall. Compare: cost of buying external CB surplus vs cost of fuel switch vs penalty rate. Choose lowest total cost.
Is residual surplus worth more than cost of banking?
Sell Bank
SELL → Enter external pool as supplier. Price at a spread above your fuel switching cost floor.
BANK → Retain for next year. Requires a separate banking verification step before 30 April.
Readiness Checklist
Pool-Ready Checklist — Before You Go to the Verifier
CB formula implemented with correct Annex II LCV and emission factors for all fuel types bunkered
MRV data reconciled — FuelEU energy dataset matches EU MRV submission; no unexplained gaps
RED/PoS documentation in place for any biofuel or RFNBO claiming actual (not default) emission factors
Ship-level CB calculated and cross-checked for each vessel in the candidate pool
Pool aggregate CB ≥ 0 confirmed mathematically before draft submitted
No ship appears in more than one pool for the same CB; no borrowing conflicts
Multi-party ISM approvals obtained or in progress — all ISM companies have reviewed and confirmed their ship allocations
Commercial settlements drafted — internal transfer pricing and external CB payment terms agreed before final pool submission
Banking verification arranged if any ship exits the pool with residual surplus CB to be carried forward
Key distinction: Phase 3 gives you verified ship-level CBs. Phase 5 gives you the verified pool. Do not attempt to finalise pool allocations (Phase 4) before Phase 3 verified CBs are in THETIS — forecast CBs and verified CBs can differ materially, and a pool built on forecast numbers may fail regulatory conditions after verification.
